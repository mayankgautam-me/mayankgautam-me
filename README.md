Embedded engineer in Delhi with more than seven years across the full embedded stack. I take hardware products from the schematic to the shelf: the board, the firmware, the cloud behind it and the app in your hand.

**Experience**

- Embedded engineer, deep-tech startup, 2024 to now: connected devices from board to cloud, including air-quality monitoring, a 5G RedCap, Wi-Fi and BLE gateway, oneM2M device platforms and Zephyr firmware on Silicon Labs Wi-Fi SoCs
- Product engineering lead, independent, 2019 to now: more than a dozen hardware products from concept to production, among them wearables, Bluetooth tags and cellular trackers, a BLDC motor controller, USB control decks, voice-capture and meditation devices and a musical instrument, with the boards, firmware, cloud back ends, SaaS and apps around them
- Senior project scientist, university research lab, 2022 to 2024: a proprietary IEEE 802.15.4 RF network stack with custom PHY and MAC layers, its GCC build system and CI/CD packaging
- Project scientist, university research lab, 2019 to 2022: Li-Fi networks on embedded Linux (Xilinx MPSoC, kernel modules, Yocto), Altium boards and OrCAD simulation
- Electronics engineer intern, 2018: wireless communication, audio processing, analog design, PCB design and assembly
- Personal builds, 2017 to 2019: 3D printers and their controller boards, self-balancing robots and a one-wheel vehicle, MIDI and HID controllers, IoT devices and computer-vision systems

**What I build**

- Products: wearables, Bluetooth and cellular trackers, IoT gateways, environmental sensors, motor controllers, control decks, instruments and robots
- Boards: KiCad and Altium, from schematic to assembled panels
- Firmware: Zephyr on nRF52, nRF54, nRF91 and SiWx917, STM32 with FreeRTOS, ESP32, Arduino, PlatformIO, USB HID, motor control (FOC)
- Wireless: 5G RedCap, BLE, LTE-M/NB-IoT, IEEE 802.15.4, Thread, Zigbee, LoRaWAN, Wi-Fi
- Networking: TCP/IP, Linux kernel networking, MQTT, oneM2M, custom PHY and MAC layers
- Embedded Linux: Yocto, kernel modules, networking stacks
- Bring-up and debug: JTAG/SWD (OpenOCD, probe-rs), GDB, logic analysers and scopes, HIL testing
- Cloud: Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, Pages, multi-tenant SaaS
- Security: device identity and provisioning, encrypted secrets (SOPS, age), hardware wallets
- AI/ML: speech recognition and speaker diarization pipelines, LLM apps on the Claude API, on-device inference, computer vision
- DevOps: Docker, CI/CD on GitHub Actions and GitLab CI, self-hosted runners, reproducible toolchains, encrypted secrets
- Apps: SwiftUI, Wear OS, Tauri desktop (Rust and Svelte), React, Next.js and Payload CMS web
- Languages: C, C++, Rust, Python, Go, TypeScript, Swift, Kotlin, shell
